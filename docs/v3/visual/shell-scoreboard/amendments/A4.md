# A4 — No “Scores hidden in settings” line on the scoreboard

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its amendments [A1](A1.md)–[A3](A3.md). No story: owner direction, 2026-10-21.

**Adds:** nothing.
**Overrides:** the ratified `scoreboard-scores-hidden-{mobile,medium,desktop}` frames and A3's frames — the line under the Games heading is gone at every pairing; the masks are unchanged.
**Repeals:** the text "Scores hidden in settings" with Hide Scores on, wherever it is drawn on this surface.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. Asked whether the footer should say "Scores hidden in settings" with Hide Scores on, the owner answered: "No, remove that from the footer". Asked whether the scoreboard's own "Scores hidden in settings" line under the Games heading should go too: "1 remove it".

- Nothing on screen says scores are hidden; the masks themselves (`–`, `▒`) are the only sign.
