## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them (2026-10-21). The owner answered the agent's numbered questions; their numbering shifted in the chat, and they restated the mapping (the owner's 1, 2, 3 and 4 are the agent's 1, 3, 5 and 6; they added that 2 and 4 are also fine), so the agent's Q1 = update notice, Q2 = offline marker, Q3 = never-viewed room offline, Q4 = standalone layout, Q5 = Install row, Q6 = ratify.

- **Update notice copy** — "An update to Baseball Theater is ready." with `Reload` and `✕` (Q1, owner: "Keep").
- **Offline marker** — the `notice-banner` under the header, no action and no dismiss, so it stays while offline; it names the copy's time and age; the footer says "Offline · saved … ago"; Hide Scores applies to offline copies exactly as online (Q2, owner: "2 and 4 are also fine").
- **Standalone layout** — the status bar takes the app background; content starts below the top safe area; the bottom safe area keeps the home indicator clear (Q4, owner: "2 and 4 are also fine").
