# A3 — Scoreboard occupants, digest and collapsed bars with Hide Scores on (S57)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its [A1](A1.md) and [A2](A2.md). Story **S57**.

**Adds:** the day digest ("Best of {date}") with Hide Scores on; the collapsed non-favorite bar with Hide Scores on.
**Overrides:** the game-row slot occupants with Hide Scores on: the pitchers of record (W, L, S) are masked; the live count, at-bat and pitcher, and the pregame win probability stay.
**Repeals:** nothing.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-21 the agent restated the drawn choices as numbered questions; the owner answered each in turn, stating the principle: "FYI the point of this is to avoid spoilers for people who want to experience the game their own way, but if they’re looking at game details they clearly want the info".

- **Pitchers of record** masked; count, at-bat and pregame win probability kept — "6: ok".
- **Collapsed bar** shows the state only — "7: ok".
- **Digest titles** are shown unchanged: "there are probably too many ways to detect this to make a reliable detector beyond the recap".
