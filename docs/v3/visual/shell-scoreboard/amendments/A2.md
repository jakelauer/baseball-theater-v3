# A2 — Navigation drawer, signed-out shell, version line, collapsed score (S56)

Amends the ratified [shell-scoreboard design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its [A1](A1.md). Story **S56**.

**Adds:** the nav drawer the burger opens on mobile and medium; the signed-out shell; the score on a collapsed (non-favorite) game bar.
**Overrides:** the desktop header — no burger, the rail is the navigation; the rail's version line — the real build version with no label (was "Tactile Beta"); the account icon's destination — Settings → Account when signed in, sign-in when signed out.
**Repeals:** the Settings nav item for signed-out users.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF — section "P — Navigation, signed-out shell, leftovers (S56, ratified 2026-10-21)", live file as edited in the S56 session (no named version).

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. The 2026-10-19 intake answers were "accept your rec" except where quoted (S56 Gap). On 2026-10-21 the agent restated the drawn choices as numbered questions; the owner answered: "For 5, a video search result should play in a modal from the results page, and a link to the game should be on the modal." and "The rest are fine".

- **Burger** — opens the nav drawer on phone and tablet and is removed on desktop, where the rail shows (2026-10-19, "accept your rec"); the drawer slides from the left over a dimmed scoreboard (2026-10-21: "The rest are fine").
- **Signed out** — "Hide settings for signed out users. Settings aren’t available without signin" (2026-10-19); the header shows "Sign in" beside the account icon (2026-10-21: "The rest are fine").
- **Version line** — the real build version with no label (2026-10-19, "accept your rec"), drawn as "v3.0.0 (c46e853)" (2026-10-21: "The rest are fine").
- **Collapsed bar** — shows the score, hidden under Hide Scores (2026-10-19, "accept your rec"); drawn as "2–1 · Bot 7" / "5–3 · Final" (2026-10-21: "The rest are fine").
