# A2 — Videos titles with Hide Scores on (S57)

Amends the ratified [game-shell design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its [A1](A1.md). Story **S57**.

**Adds:** the recap video's title "Recap (score hidden)" on the game page with Hide Scores on.
**Overrides:** the ratified `game-hidden-score-mobile` frame's rewritten video titles ("Judge 447-ft HR"): every title other than the recap's shows as MLB writes it.
**Repeals:** nothing.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them. On 2026-10-21 the agent restated the drawn choices as numbered questions; the owner answered each in turn, stating the principle: "FYI the point of this is to avoid spoilers for people who want to experience the game their own way, but if they’re looking at game details they clearly want the info".

- **Recap video title** — "The game recap video should have its title changed to “Recap (score hidden)” on the game page."
- **Other video titles** are not rewritten: "there are probably too many ways to detect this to make a reliable detector beyond the recap".
- **Search** is out of this rule: "Search is unaffected by hide scores".
