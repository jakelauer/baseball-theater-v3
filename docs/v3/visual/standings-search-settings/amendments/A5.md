# A5 — The deleted-account page (S86)

Amends the ratified [standings-search-settings design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its [A1](A1.md)–[A4](A4.md). Story **S86**.

**Adds:** the Settings page after an account is deleted: signed out, blank but for "Your account and your data are deleted.", with no dismiss and no action, at mobile and desktop.
**Overrides:** A4's `scoreboard-deleted-mobile` (the signed-out scoreboard with a dismissible notice) — after deletion the app shows this page at `/settings` instead. A4's file and frame stay sealed in its manifest; this amendment supersedes that one frame.
**Repeals:** the dismissible "Your account and your data are deleted." notice on the scoreboard.

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/wqGKEFlvyxq0ObWZhRJdPF — the frames `settings-deleted-mobile` and `settings-deleted-desktop` below A4's row (S86, ratified 2026-10-29), live file as edited in the S86 session (no named version).

## Frames

Every export is a 1× PNG of the Figma frame with the same name, built from shell-scoreboard A2's `shell-signed-out-*` frames.

| Export | Pairing | What it shows |
| ------ | ------- | ------------- |
| `settings-deleted-mobile.png` | mobile | The signed-out header ("Sign in" beside the account icon) over an otherwise blank page with one centered line, "Your account and your data are deleted." — no dismiss, no action, no footer. |
| `settings-deleted-desktop.png` | desktop | The same beside the signed-out nav rail (no Settings item, nothing active); the line centered in the page area — no dismiss, no action. |

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- **The deleted-account page** (2026-10-29, on seeing S79's capture of A4's notice): "I don't think there is a visual for the deleted display, or at least I didn't see one. But regardless, the deletion message should be on an otherwise blank page. Not dismissable. It should be the settings page, and it should be triggered by visiting the settings page with a server-only cookie that is only sent to users after deletion."
- **How long it shows** (2026-10-29, asked how long visiting `/settings` keeps showing it): "Until seen once". The server clears its cookie in the response that reports it (S87).

## Interpretation

The agent's reading of things the frames show that no decision states, for the owner to confirm by ratifying.

- *Agent:* the page keeps the app's signed-out chrome (header, and the rail on desktop) so the reader can still go somewhere; "blank" applies to the page area.
- *Agent:* the line is Light, 16px, centered horizontally, a little above the vertical middle; there is no heading and no footer.
- *Agent:* on desktop the rail marks nothing active, because Settings has no rail item when signed out.
