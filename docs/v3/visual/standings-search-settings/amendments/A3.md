# A3 — Signed-in Account section and Patreon linking (S58)

Amends the ratified [standings-search-settings design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its [A1](A1.md) and [A2](A2.md). Story **S58**.

**Adds:** the Settings Account section signed in — "Signed in as {email}" and a "Sign out" link; Patreon not linked and linked with its tier, with "Unlink Patreon".
**Overrides:** the ratified Account group ("Sign in with magic link" button, "Connect Patreon") wherever it is drawn, including A1's `settings-install-*` and A2's `settings-install-ios-mobile` frames. Settings is signed-in only (ADR-027), so the signed-out Account group is unreachable.
**Repeals:** the "Sign in with magic link" action and the ratified DESIGN.md's magic-link sign-in. BT draws no sign-in, email or code-entry screen: Logto's own sign-in experience provides them, and the header's "Sign in" (shell-scoreboard A2) hands off to it.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- **Sign-in screens** — 2026-10-22: "S58 sign in design can be placeholder for the actual sign in screen. I will use the logto features for that. We can do the rest of it though". No sign-in, email or code-entry frame is drawn; Logto's sign-in experience provides them.
- On 2026-10-22 the agent restated the drawn choices as four numbered questions; the owner answered:
    - **Sign out** — "Text link" (not the red button the drafts reused).
    - **Signed-in line** — "Signed in as {email}": "Yes".
    - **Patreon wording** — both lines as drafted: "Keep".
    - **Unlinking** — "Unlink from bt": linked users can unlink from BT, not only manage on Patreon.
