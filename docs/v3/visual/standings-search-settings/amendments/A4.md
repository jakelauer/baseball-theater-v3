# A4 — Download and delete your data (S78)

Amends the ratified [standings-search-settings design](../DESIGN.md) (ratified 2026-10-14) and its [A1](A1.md)–[A3](A3.md). Story **S78**.

**Adds:** two ways in on the Settings Account group, "Download your data" and "Delete account"; a stored download with its link and expiry; the twice-in-24-hours refusal; the delete dialog with its typed phrase, its download warning, the re-sign-in prompt and a failed deletion with "Try again" and the support message; the signed-out scoreboard after deletion with its notice.
**Overrides:** A3's Account group, which gains the two ways in below "Connect Patreon" (A3's lines are unchanged).
**Repeals:** nothing.

## Decisions

Recorded as the owner stated them.

- **Download and delete are separate** (2026-10-27): "Deleting and downloading aren't connected. If someone wants to delete their data, they can do it without downloading. If they want to download, they can do it without deleting. If they want to do both, they have to download first, then delete."
- **Downloads are kept 14 days** (2026-10-27): "The download should be hosted in R2 with an expiration (ideally a bucket age-out policy of 14 days)." "They can request a download twice per day", counted over "Rolling 24 hours".
- **Linked in Settings and by email** (2026-10-27, asked how the file reaches the user): "Both".
- **Deleted with the account** (2026-10-27): "Delete them together with a warning shown and a link to the download."
- **Immediate, heavily verified** (2026-10-23): "Immediately, but with maximal verification including typing a phrase". The phrase is the server's `delete my account`.
- **A failed deletion** (2026-10-27): "Try Again, contact support is failed repeatedly". Then (2026-10-28): "I want a message to the user to show all the time, which tells them "Contact support if it fails repeatedly" (but with better wording)", and "mailto: support@baseball.theater". So there is no failure count: the support message shows with every failure.

## Interpretation

The agent's reading of things the frames show that no decision states, for the owner to confirm by ratifying.

- *Agent:* the ways in are text links in the Account group, like "Sign out"; "Delete account" is Primary Light, the red for text on Dark.
- *Agent:* deletion opens a dialog over Settings rather than a new page; download needs no dialog.
- *Agent:* the expiry and the next allowed time are the server's dates (`expiresAt`, `nextAllowedAt`) shown as dates, with no client date math.
- *Agent:* the limit refusal is Primary Light, like other refusals; the failed deletion reuses the room error's two lines ("Couldn't …", "Check your connection and try again.").
- *Agent:* the support message wording is "If this keeps happening, email support@baseball.theater." — the owner asked for better wording than the placeholder.
- *Agent:* after deletion the app is signed out and lands on the scoreboard, since Settings is signed-in only; the notice is a message with ✕ and no action.
- *Agent:* the deleted notice in `scoreboard-deleted-mobile` is a detached copy of `notice-banner` with "Reload" removed, because the component has no action-less variant.
