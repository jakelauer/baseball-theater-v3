**Gap:** S81's pilot photographed S60's shell at the six frames it builds (shell-scoreboard A2's `nav-drawer-mobile`, `nav-drawer-medium`, `shell-signed-out-mobile`, `shell-signed-out-desktop`, `shell-version-desktop`, and A5's `scoreboard-header-version-desktop`; captures in `docs/v3/design-checks/S60/`), and the owner judged them a mismatch (2026-10-28, the "S60 re-verified" entry in [AUDIT](./AUDIT.md)): *"#1 can be fixed by including this: <link rel="stylesheet" href="https://use.typekit.net/weo6zjs.css"> which adds `clarendon-urw` as an available font (which is what the BT in the logo is set to use). I suspect the other text-related ones are either font-size or font-face mismatches with the Figma design, or web rendering differences from Figma. Make sure those match first. If they match and there are still issues, let me know. Fix the color issue."* The differences shown to the owner before that verdict: (1) the "BT" of the wordmark is IBM Plex Sans in the build (`web/src/layout/Wordmark.tsx`) and the slab-serif `clarendon-urw` in the frames; (2) the active rail item is filled with Mantine's darker dark-scheme shade of `primary` with no marker, while the frames fill it Primary `#CE0F0F` with a lighter bar at its left edge; (3) the rail items are 14px text with a 16px inset in the build and 16px text, 44px rows and a 24px inset in the frames; (4) the header's "Sign in" and the version line are dimmed in the build and Light in the frames; (5) the footer follows the content in the build and sits at the bottom of the screen in the frames. S81 AC 7: each mismatch the owner names becomes a follow-up story in the next backlog review. Inserted by the 2026-10-28 backlog review.

**Impact:** Before S83, the frame around every page (the BT logo, the side menu, the header) looked close to the approved design but not the same: the logo letters were in the wrong typeface and the highlighted menu item was a duller red. S83 impact: the logo, the menu and the header text look the way the owner designed them on phone, tablet and desktop, and the owner has checked that by eye.
