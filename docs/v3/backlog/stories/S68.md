**Gap:** Gap-map rows (c) and (d) and the FEATURES carry row "hide scores": collapsed bars (`207:1982`), `–` score cells, `▒▒-▒▒` records are drawn; none is built. S57 (`shell-scoreboard/amendments/A3.md`) masks the final slot's pitchers of record and draws collapsed bars with the state only; A4 removes every "Scores hidden in settings" line (owner, 2026-10-21: "1 remove it"), so the masks are the only sign. The collapsed bar shows the score unless Hide Scores is on (owner, 2026-10-19; S56 draws it). Whether scores are hidden is decided by a function, never by reading the setting directly, so the setting can grow past an on/off switch without touching its readers (owner, 2026-10-21: "establish the “should scores be hidden” value through a function rather than directly through the setting. This makes it easier to make the setting more complex than a Boolean toggle in the future"). Inserted by the 2026-10-19 design intake review (two independent fresh-context reviewers, merged).

**Impact:** Before S68, every game took the same space and every score was always visible. S68 impact: games without your favorite teams fold into slim bars you can tap open, and one switch hides every score and record for fans catching up later.
