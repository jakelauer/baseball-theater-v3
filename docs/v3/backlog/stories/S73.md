**Gap:** ADR-023: a web app manifest with icons from the flag mark, `display: standalone`, a `vite-plugin-pwa` service worker registered at scope `/` (so later Web Push attaches without migration), a precached shell, a waiting worker that surfaces "update available", the quiet Install app row in Settings, and safe-area insets in standalone mode. None exists. **S55 ratified 2026-10-21** (`shell-scoreboard/amendments/A1.md`, `standings-search-settings/amendments/A1.md`): the status bar takes the app background (`#10100F`), content sits below the top safe area and above the home indicator; the update notice reads "An update to Baseball Theater is ready."; the **App** group sits above Account and is hidden once installed and where the browser cannot install. iOS (owner, 2026-10-21, answering the 2026-10-21 design intake): "iOS should show the share instructions" — the row is not hidden on iOS Safari; its frame is drawn in S56. Inserted by the 2026-10-19 design intake review (two independent fresh-context reviewers, merged).

**Impact:** Before S73, Baseball Theater could only be used in a browser tab and loaded from scratch each visit. S73 impact: it installs to the home screen like an app, opens instantly on return visits, and says quietly when an update is ready.
