**Gap:** S57 ratified the game rooms and Videos with Hide Scores on (`game-rooms/amendments/A3.md`, `A4.md`; `game-shell/amendments/A2.md`, `A3.md`). The owner's principle: "the point of this is to avoid spoilers for people who want to experience the game their own way, but if they’re looking at game details they clearly want the info". Live hides the score ("Hide the score"); Plays hides the linescore ("Hide the score and any innings, and R/H/E") and keeps the batter's line; the Runs view hides the score after each play; Box is unchanged; the recap is readable under the heading "Recap"; on the game page only the recap video is retitled "Recap (score hidden)"; no text says scores are hidden. The rooms (S3, S41, S10, S64) and video cards (S69) all land before S68 builds `useScoresHidden` and `HiddenValue`, so their hidden states are one story after both, not five re-scopes ahead of the hook. Upstream marks the recap clip with the keywords `game-recap` / `MLBCOM_GAME_RECAP` (`fixtures/raw/content-823823.json`). Inserted by the 2026-10-22 design intake review (two independent fresh-context reviewers, merged).

**Impact:** Before S75, Hide Scores protected only the scoreboard; opening a game's Live or Plays tab or its videos gave the result away. S75 impact: a fan can follow a game's pitches and plays without seeing the score, while the box score and recap stay there for anyone who wants them.
