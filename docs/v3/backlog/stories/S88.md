**Gap:** S84 left two ways to find a story's shipping commit: `story_tip` in `scripts/lib/checks.sh` takes the newest commit whose subject starts `<ID>: `, while `shipping_commit` in `scripts/lib/reverify.sh` reads the ledger (the commit that adds the story's completion entry, or a backfill's `Shipped in`). They already disagree for S7 and S14, V3's ledger commit is a later "V3 done" commit, and the subject rule depends on every checkpoint being worded correctly forever. Asked whether one lookup should replace the other, and accepting the recommendation to use the ledger, the owner answered (2026-10-29): *"Yes"*. Inserted by the owner, 2026-10-29.

**Impact:** Before S88, a story's proof could look at a slightly different set of changes depending on which of two lookups ran. S88 impact: every proof finds "the commit this story shipped in" the same way, from the record of what shipped.
