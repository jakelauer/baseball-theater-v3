**Gap:** ADR-028 gives signed-in users a way to download their data and a separate way to delete it, immediately, after maximal verification including typing a phrase (owner, 2026-10-23; separated by the owner 2026-10-27: *"Deleting and downloading aren't connected."*). A download is kept 14 days, can be requested twice in any rolling 24 hours, and is linked in Settings and by email; deleting the account deletes it, *"with a warning shown and a link to the download"* (owner, 2026-10-27). The ratified Settings Account section (standings-search-settings A3, S58) has "Signed in as {email}", "Sign out" and the Patreon rows, but no frame draws a way in to downloading or deletion, the current download with its expiry, the request limit, the typed-phrase confirmation, the re-sign-in a stale session needs, or what the user sees once their account is gone. A UI story can't draw a missing screen in code (rule 18). The next free standings-search-settings amendment is **A4**. Inserted by the owner, 2026-10-23.

**Impact:** Before S78, there was no approved picture of how someone deletes their data. S78 impact: the owner has approved each step — getting a download, confirming a deletion by typing a phrase, and what happens after — so it can be built exactly as drawn.
