**Gap:** A4 (S78) draws what someone sees after deleting their account as the signed-out scoreboard with a dismissible notice (`scoreboard-deleted-mobile`). Reviewing S79, the owner redirected it (2026-10-29): *"I don't think there is a visual for the deleted display, or at least I didn't see one. But regardless, the deletion message should be on an otherwise blank page. Not dismissable. It should be the settings page, and it should be triggered by visiting the settings page with a server-only cookie that is only sent to users after deletion."* Asked how long the message shows: *"Until seen once"*. No ratified frame draws that page, and a UI story can't draw a missing screen in code (rule 18). The next free standings-search-settings amendment is **A5**. Inserted by the owner, 2026-10-29.

**Impact:** Before S86, the approved picture of "your account is gone" was a small banner over the scoreboard that could be closed. S86 impact: the owner approves a plain page saying the account and its data are deleted, so S79 can build exactly that.
